# FalconX

> FalconX is an institutional digital-asset prime brokerage providing trading, credit and financing, custody, and market-access services to banks, asset managers, hedge funds, and other financial institutions. It offers spot, derivatives, options, and FX execution across 400+ tokens via an OTC desk, an execution-management system (Execute), and direct market access (DMA) with cross-margining and one-click borrowing. FalconX exposes a REST trading API at api.falconx.io for price quotes, order execution, trade history, balances, transfers, and trade limits.
